Signing in

Is signing in safe? Does the app see my password?

You sign in on Riot's own page (auth.riotgames.com) inside the app. Your password goes directly to Riot – the app never sees or stores it. It only keeps the session Riot gives it, in your device's secure storage.

Why do I have to sign in again sometimes?

Riot ends sessions from time to time, for example after a password change, a long break or a security check. The app then asks you to sign in again; your wishlist and history stay.

Will my friends see me as online?

No. The app reads your friends list but tells Riot chat that you are offline.

Store, wishlist and notifications

I don't get wishlist or night market notifications.

Allow notifications for Val Companion in your device settings. On iPhone also turn on Background App Refresh (Settings → General → Background App Refresh). On Android, set battery usage for the app to “Unrestricted”. The system decides when background checks run, so a notification can come a little after the store resets – opening the app always checks immediately.

How do I add a widget?

iPhone: touch and hold the home screen → Edit → Add Widget → Val Companion. Android: touch and hold the home screen → Widgets → Val Companion. Open the app once after signing in so the widget gets its data.

Premium and subscriptions

How do I restore Premium on a new phone or after reinstalling?

Sign in with the same Apple ID / Google account you bought it with, then in the app tap your name at the top → Premium → Restore purchases.

How do I cancel my subscription?

iPhone: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Val Companion. Android: Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Or in the app: your name → Premium → Manage subscription. Premium stays active until the end of the paid period. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple and Google, so refunds are requested there: reportaproblem.apple.com (App Store) or Google Play refunds. We can't issue refunds ourselves.

I paid but Premium isn't unlocked.

First try Restore purchases (your name → Premium). If that doesn't help, email us your Support ID: in the Premium screen, touch and hold Restore purchases for 5 seconds until the Support ID appears, then tap it to copy it. The Support ID is an anonymous number – it contains no personal or Riot account data.
