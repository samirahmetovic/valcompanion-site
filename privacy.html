Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data the app Val Companion (iOS and Android) and this website process, why, and what rights you have. In short:

  • There is no Val Companion server. The app talks directly to Riot Games and stores everything else on your device.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no selling of data.
  • Your Riot password is entered on Riot's own sign-in page; the app never sees it.
  • Subscriptions are bought through Apple or Google and managed with RevenueCat using an anonymous ID.

1. Controller

Responsible for data processing under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

Samir Ahmetovic
Email: samirahmetovic6@gmail.com

No data protection officer has been appointed, as there is no legal obligation to do so.

2. Signing in with Riot

To use the app you sign in with your Riot account. The sign-in happens on Riot's own web page (auth.riotgames.com), shown inside the app. You enter your credentials there, directly with Riot; the app does not see or store your password.

After signing in, Riot gives the app access tokens and session cookies. They are stored in your device's secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore-encrypted storage) so you stay signed in, and are sent only to Riot. The same applies to further accounts you add.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR – necessary to provide the app you chose to use.

3. Game data from Riot Games

With your session, the app requests data directly from Riot Games' servers (e.g. *.riotgames.com, *.pvp.net). This includes:

  • account data: Riot ID (name and tag), player ID (PUUID), region and shard;
  • your daily store, night market, bundles and wallet balances (VP, Radianite, Kingdom Credits);
  • your collection and loadout – changes you make to your loadout in the app are sent to Riot;
  • rank, rating and match history, including match details such as rounds, kills and positions;
  • your friends list and their status, via Riot's chat service. The app tells Riot chat that you are offline, so it does not make you appear online.

Match data naturally also contains the Riot IDs and game statistics of other players who were in the same matches. The app uses them only to show you match details and features such as “seen before” and head-to-head stats, and keeps them only on your device.

When your device connects to Riot, Riot receives your IP address and device information. Riot processes this under its own responsibility; see the Riot Games Privacy Notice. Val Companion is not affiliated with Riot Games.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR for your own data; Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR for other players' data (legitimate interest in showing the matches you played).

4. Game images and names (valorant-api.com)

Pictures, videos and names of weapons, skins, agents, maps and ranks are loaded from the community service valorant-api.com / media.valorant-api.com. No account data is sent – but, as with every internet request, your IP address and technical information (device type, app version) reach that service. Images are then cached on your device.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR – legitimate interest in showing game content without operating our own server.

5. Data stored on your device

The following stays on your device and is not sent to us:

  • sign-in data of your accounts (secure storage, see section 2);
  • a local match archive, store history, players you met, rank goals and session data;
  • your wishlist, settings and premium status;
  • data for home screen widgets (store, wallet, rank and their images), shared with the widget via the app's own storage area.

Signing out of an account in the app removes its sign-in, wishlist and history from the device; cached match details are cleaned up afterwards. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

6. Background updates and notifications

If you allow notifications, the app checks your store in the background (iOS Background App Refresh / Android WorkManager) and shows local notifications – e.g. when an item from your wishlist is in your store or the night market starts. These notifications are created on your device; no push service and no server of ours is involved. Widgets and Siri / Shortcuts actions refresh the same way. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.

7. Sharing and saving images

Share cards (e.g. your store or stats) are created on your device. They leave your device only if you share them yourself via the system share sheet – then the app you choose processes them. Saving to your photo library needs your permission and only adds images; the app does not read your photos.

8. Premium subscription (Apple, Google, RevenueCat)

Payment: subscriptions are bought through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google; we do not receive your name, email or payment details. Their privacy policies apply: Apple, Google.

Subscription management: to check whether your subscription is active, the app uses RevenueCat, Inc. (USA), as a processor. RevenueCat processes:

  • a random, anonymous app user ID created on your device (shown in the app as “Support ID”);
  • purchase information from Apple / Google (product, price, currency, purchase and renewal dates, transaction IDs, store country);
  • technical data (IP address, operating system and app version, device language).

Your Riot account is not linked to RevenueCat. RevenueCat is based in the USA; the transfer is based on EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR) in RevenueCat's data processing agreement. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR – performance of the subscription. Purchase records are kept as long as needed for the subscription and as required by commercial and tax law.

9. App store statistics and crash reports

Apple and Google provide app developers with aggregated download statistics. If you have agreed in your device settings to share analytics or diagnostics with app developers, Apple / Google also provide us with anonymous crash reports. This is controlled solely by your device settings. The app itself contains no analytics SDK.

When you visit it, GitHub processes your IP address and technical request data (server logs), e.g. for security. The website uses no cookies, no analytics and no external fonts or scripts. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR – legitimate interest in providing the website securely. GitHub is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR). See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

11. Contacting us

If you email us, we process your email address and message to answer you (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR) and delete them once your request is settled, unless retention is required by law.

12. How long data is kept

  • On your device: until you sign out of that account or delete the app.
  • RevenueCat: as long as your subscription data is needed and as required by law; on request we delete the data linked to your Support ID (see Delete your data).
  • Emails: until your request is settled.

13. Your rights

You have the right to access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21). Just send an email to the address above.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), e.g. the authority responsible for us: Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen, Kavalleriestr. 2–4, 40213 Düsseldorf, Germany, ldi.nrw.de.

There is no automated decision-making or profiling within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR.

14. Children

The app is meant for VALORANT players who meet Riot's age requirements. It does not knowingly collect data from children beyond what is described above.
