Val Companion

An unofficial companion app for VALORANT on iPhone and Android.

Check your daily store and night market, keep a wishlist with alerts, follow your rank and match history, see your friends' status and put your store on the home screen with widgets. Premium adds deeper stats, more widgets and more accounts as a monthly or yearly subscription.
