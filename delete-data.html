Delete your data

Val Companion by Samir Ahmetovic

Val Companion has no accounts of its own and no server: you sign in with your Riot account, and the app's data is stored on your device. Here's how to remove everything.

1. Data on your device

  • One account: tap your name at the top of the app → tap the sign-out icon next to the account → Sign out. The app deletes that account's sign-in, wishlist, match history and store history; cached match details are cleaned up automatically afterwards.
  • Everything: delete the app from your device. All its data, including the widgets', is removed.

This cannot be undone.

2. Subscription data at RevenueCat

If you have bought Premium, RevenueCat stores an anonymous ID with your purchase history (see the Privacy Policy). To have it deleted, email samirahmetovic6@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion” and your Support ID (Premium screen → touch and hold Restore purchases for 5 seconds → tap the ID to copy it). We delete it within 30 days. Records we must keep by law (e.g. for tax purposes) are kept only as long as required.

Deleting data does not cancel a subscription – cancel it first in your Apple or Google account (see Support).

3. Your Riot account

Your Riot account and game data belong to Riot Games. To delete them, use Riot's own process: Riot Support.
